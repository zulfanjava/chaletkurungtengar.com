Policies & Information

Cancellation / No Show

Single Bookings:

Notify us at least 10 days before arrival to avoid charge of one-night stay.

Group Bookings:

21 days cancellation notice required. No-show or early check-out: one night's rate charged.

Deposit & Payment

Single Booking:

Full payment required at reservation. One modification allowed (contact Khadijah 012-9097591).

Group Booking:

50% deposit required. One modification allowed. Contact office to discuss changes.

Refund & Postpone

Notice period: 10 days prior to arrival for refund eligibility.

No refund for cancellation after 10 days.

Postpone allowed (subject to availability).

Check In & Check Out

Check-in: 15:00 – 21:00 (RM20/hr early check-in subject to availability).
Check-out: 12:00
Late check-out:
Until 18:00 → extra 50% of existing rate
After 18:00 → full night charge
Prior notification required. Subject to room availability.

For any modifications or inquiries: Khadijah (012-9097591) – Only one modification allowed per booking.
