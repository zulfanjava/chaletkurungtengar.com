Cancellation / No show

  • Single booking: notify at least 10 days before arrival to avoid 1‑night charge.
  • Group booking: notify at least 21 days before arrival to avoid 1‑night charge.
  • No Show or Early Check Out: one night's rate will be charged.

Deposit Policy

  • Single booking: Full payment required upon reservation.
  • Group booking: 50% deposit required.
  • Only ONE modification allowed per booking.

Refund Policy

  • Cancellation with ≥10 days notice (single) / 21 days (group): refund eligible.
